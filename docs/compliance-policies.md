## 1. Terms and Conditions

**Last Updated:** October 2026

Welcome to the official portal of the **Tennis Ball Kadapa Premier League (T-KPL)**, accessible at our official website. By registering as a player, submitting personal details, or paying the registration fee, you agree to comply with and be bound by the following terms and conditions:

### 1.1 Tournament Overview & Organizers
* The tournament is organized and managed by **Sultan Sports (Kadapa)** represented by Mansoor, in partnership with **NR Sports (Vempalli)** represented by Sunil.
* All matches are played with official **Guru Laser Tennis Balls** in Kadapa, Andhra Pradesh, and are broadcast live on YouTube and social media channels.

### 1.2 Player Eligibility & Verification
* **Jurisdiction:** Registration is open strictly to residents of **New Kadapa** and **Old Kadapa** (YSR Kadapa District, Andhra Pradesh).
* **Age Requirement:** Applicants must be between **15 to 50 years of age** at the time of registration.
* **Identity Proof:** Every registrant must present valid government photo identification (such as an Aadhaar Card, Driving License, or Voter ID) during physical scrutiny or jersey kit distribution.
* **Scrutiny & Disqualification:** The organizing committee reserves the sole right to reject or disqualify any application found with falsified age, incorrect locality details, or duplicate entries.

### 1.3 Registration, Ground Selection Trials & Auction Process
* **Stage 1 — Online Registration:** Submitting the form and paying the **₹399 registration fee** confirms your slot for the official **T-KPL On-Ground Selection Trials**. Registration does not place a player directly into the auction pool.
* **Stage 2 — Ground Selection Trials (Skill Test):**
  * All registered players will be called for on-ground trials in Kadapa (tentatively scheduled starting mid-November, approx. 14 November 2026 onwards, with exact venue and slot timings notified via WhatsApp/SMS).
  * **Skill Evaluation Format:**
    * **Bowlers:** Get to bowl 1 full over (6 deliveries) under umpire and selector scrutiny to demonstrate pace, accuracy, and variations.
    * **Batsmen:** Get a dedicated batting slot to showcase shot selection, defense, and power hitting.
    * **All-Rounders & Wicket-Keepers:** Evaluated on their combined primary and secondary cricket skills.
  * Only players who clear this physical trial and meet performance standards will be shortlisted by the selection committee.
* **Stage 3 — Final Auction Pool & Team Selection:**
  * Players shortlisted from the Selection Trials are officially entered into the **Final T-KPL Player Auction Pool**.
  * Entry into the auction pool **does not guarantee selection** into a franchise squad. Team selection is determined strictly by franchise owners during the live bidding process.
  * Selected players in the final squads will receive:
    * 1 Official Full Team Kit (Shirt + Trouser)
    * 1 Official Practice / Additional T-shirt
    * Official match participation under the assigned franchise team

### 1.4 Code of Conduct & Sportsmanship
* All players are required to maintain the highest standards of sportsmanship, discipline, and respect towards umpires, match referees, opponents, and tournament organizers.
* Any indiscipline, on-field misconduct, use of abusive language, or violation of tournament rules will lead to immediate disqualification and a ban from future seasons without any refund.

### 1.5 Media & Broadcast Rights
* By registering, players grant the T-KPL organizing committee the perpetual right to photograph, video record, live stream, and publish their likeness, match statistics, and team photos across YouTube, social media, banners, and promotional material without additional compensation.

### 1.6 Limitation of Liability
* Cricket is a physical sport. While organizers make standard first-aid arrangements at the venue, T-KPL, Sultan Sports, NR Sports, and ground authorities are not liable for any sports injuries, physical accidents, or loss of personal belongings during the trials, auction, or match fixtures.

## 2. Cancellation and Refund Policy

**Last Updated:** October 2026

At T-KPL, we believe in complete transparency with our players and franchise owners. Please read our policy regarding cancellations and refunds carefully before making any online payment:

### 2.1 Player Registration Fee (₹399/-)
* The registration fee of **₹399 INR** covers participation in the official **On-Ground Selection Trials** (including ground booking, Guru Laser match balls, selector/umpire scrutiny, profile processing, and system infrastructure).
* **Non-Refundable Policy:** Once a player completes registration and payment is confirmed, the registration fee is **strictly non-refundable**. Specifically:
  * **Trial Non-Qualification:** If a player is evaluated at the Selection Trials and is not shortlisted for the Final Auction Pool, the fee is **not refundable**.
  * **Auction Outcome:** If a qualified player is not picked or purchased by any franchise team during the live auction, the fee is **not refundable**.
  * **Trial Absence:** If a player fails to attend or reports late to their assigned selection trial slot on the ground, the fee is **not refundable**.

### 2.2 Situations Where Refunds Are Issued
A full refund will be provided under the following exceptional circumstances:
1. **Tournament Cancellation:** If the tournament is officially cancelled or abandoned by the organizers before the auction takes place and is not rescheduled, 100% of the registration fee will be refunded.
2. **Duplicate or Excess Payment:** If a player is debited twice for the same registration due to a network glitch or multiple button presses, the duplicate amount will be refunded in full.

### 2.3 Refund Request & Processing Time
* **Reporting Window:** For duplicate or failed payment deductions, players must report the issue within **7 days** of transaction by emailing `syed516.sm@gmail.com` or messaging the support lines with their Registration ID and Razorpay Payment ID.
* **Processing Time:** Once verified and approved by the organizing committee, refunds are processed through our payment partner (Razorpay) back to the original payment source (UPI account or bank account) within **5 to 7 business working days**.
* No cash refunds will be issued for online transactions.

## 3. Privacy Policy

**Last Updated:** October 2026

The T-KPL organizing committee is committed to protecting the personal information and privacy of every cricket player.

### 3.1 Information We Collect
When you register for T-KPL, we collect:
* **Personal Identification:** Full Name, Date of Birth, and Father's/Guardian's Name.
* **Contact Information:** Mobile Phone Number and Emergency Contact Number.
* **Geographical Details:** Locality, Ward, or Town within Kadapa.
* **Cricket Profile:** Batting hand, bowling style, playing role, and prior tournament experience.
* **Photographs:** Player portrait photograph (used exclusively for your player ID card, official digital receipt, and the live auction display banner).
* **Payment Records:** Transaction reference IDs and Razorpay payment timestamps (we **never** store sensitive banking passwords, UPI PINs, or card details).

### 3.2 How We Use Your Information
* To verify eligibility and residency in Kadapa.
* To generate your official downloadable digital payment receipt and unique QR code.

### 3.3 Data Protection & Storage
* All player records and uploaded photographs are stored securely on encrypted cloud servers (Supabase) with restricted access limited strictly to authorized tournament administrators.
* **We do not sell, rent, trade, or share your personal details with third-party advertising or marketing agencies.**
